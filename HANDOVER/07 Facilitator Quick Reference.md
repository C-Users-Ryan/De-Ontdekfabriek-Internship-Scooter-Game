# Facilitator Quick Reference: Run-of-Show and Troubleshooting
**For:** De Ontdekfabriek staff running the Kenya Scooter activity on the tablet
**What it is:** Two one-page cards to print or laminate. The first is the daily run-of-show; the second is a symptom to fix card. Both match the current build (the in-build settings menu, the locked landscape, the new facilitator actions).
**Companion to:** the fuller [Facilitator Guide](06%20Facilitator%20Guide.md) (pedagogy and debrief). Where the two differ on setup or troubleshooting, THIS card is current.


---

## How to open the settings menu (you need this for several fixes)

There is no visible settings button, so children cannot reach it. To open it:
- **On the tablet:** press and **hold the top-left corner of the screen for 2 seconds**. The game pauses and the menu opens.
- **On a computer (testing):** press **F8**.
- To close: **KLAAR** (bottom right). Changes are saved and stay saved.

# Card 1: Daily run-of-show

## Start of the day (about 2 minutes)
- Power on the tablet. **Open the game** by tapping its icon on the home screen if it is not already open.
- **Lock the game so children cannot leave it (screen-pinning).** Open the recent-apps view (the square button, or swipe up and hold), tap the game's icon at the top of its card, and choose **Pin / Vastzetten**. Now the Home, Back and Recents buttons stay inside the game. To unlock at the end of the day, hold **Back + Recents** together. (Full setup: the tablet-setup checklist.)
- The screen is **landscape and stays landscape** (it is locked, it will not flip).
- Turn the **volume** up with the side buttons. Check the **brightness** is high enough for the room.
- Keep a **charger** nearby. Playing while charging is fine.
- **New class or new day?** Clear the old scores: open settings (hold top-left 2 s) then **BEHEER** then **Klassement wissen** (tap, then tap again to confirm). Skip this if the same class continues.

## Running a group
1. On the start screen, **tap a team name** to begin (the screen says *TIK EEN NAAM OM TE SPELEN*). Any name simply starts the game.
2. Hand the tablet to the first player held **flat-ish in two hands, landscape**. They **tilt the tablet** to steer, press the **right** side to go faster and the **left** side to brake. A short *KANTEL OM TE STUREN* hint appears for new players; you do not need to explain more.
3. A turn lasts about 2 minutes. At the **charge station** the turn score and the class standings appear. Tap **Volgende speler** to pass the tablet to the next teammate. The last player sees the **eindstand**.
4. Next group: tap **Nieuwe groep**.

## Between groups (optional)
Open settings (hold top-left 2 s) to adjust how the game plays: difficulty, how much traffic, time of day, length of a turn, and more. Everything you change **stays saved** for the next group. To undo everything: **Alles terug naar standaard** (bottom left of the menu).

## End of the day
- Optionally clear the scores (settings then **BEHEER** then **Klassement wissen**).
- Plug the tablet in to charge.

# Card 2: Troubleshooting (symptom to fix)

Most fixes start by opening the settings menu: **hold the top-left corner for 2 seconds**.

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Steering feels crooked or off-centre** | Settings then **BESTURING** then **KALIBREER**. Hold the tablet the way the player should hold it, then tap. This re-zeroes the steering. (Gyro drift is the most common real complaint, this fixes it.) |
| **Steering does not respond at all** | The tablet must not be lying flat, and its motion sensor must be on (Android settings). Close and reopen the app if it persists. |
| **Left and right feel reversed** | Settings then **BESTURING** then turn **Sturen omkeren** on. |
| **A child feels dizzy or motion-sick** | Settings then **BESTURING** then lower **Bewegingsgevoeligheid**. |
| **The game froze or is not responding** | Wait about 10 seconds (it can pause on a transition). Still stuck: close and reopen the app. Still stuck: restart the tablet. **The scores are saved and will not be lost.** |
| **No sound** | Turn the tablet volume up with the side buttons. Then settings then **GELUID** and raise **Hoofdvolume**, **Muziekvolume** or **Omgevingsgeluid**. |
| **The screen is too dark** | Raise the tablet brightness (swipe down from the top). Turn off automatic brightness so it does not dim itself. |
| **Too easy, or too hard** | Settings then **MOEILIJKHEID** and **VERKEER EN WEG** (how many cars, pedestrians, how strict). |
| **The game settings got messed up** | Settings then **Alles terug naar standaard** (bottom left) puts everything back to how it shipped. |
| **Scores need clearing for a new class** | Settings then **BEHEER** then **Klassement wissen**. Tap, then tap again to confirm. This cannot be undone, so only do it between classes. |
| **The screen is the wrong way round** | The game is locked to landscape. If it still looks wrong, close and reopen the app. |
| **The Home/Back buttons do nothing, you cannot leave the game** | This is on purpose: the game is **pinned** so children stay in it. To leave, hold **Back + Recents** together (enter the tablet unlock code if asked). To put the lock back, pin it again (see Start of the day). |
| **The game is not pinned / a child reached the Android home screen** | Pin it again: open recent apps, tap the game's icon, choose **Pin / Vastzetten** (see Start of the day). |
| **A child does not know how to steer** | The *KANTEL OM TE STUREN* hint shows by itself. Tell them: "tilt the tablet like a steering wheel, the way you want to go." |

### If nothing else works
Restart the tablet (hold power, then Restart). The game opens to the start screen. The leaderboard and your settings are saved, so nothing is lost.
