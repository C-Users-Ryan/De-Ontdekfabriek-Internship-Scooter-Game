# Handover: Facilitator Acceptance Test (15 Minutes)

**For:** Ryan to run once with one real De Ontdekfabriek facilitator, before the internship ends.
**What it is:** a scripted, watch-do-not-help test that turns "the facilitator docs and menu should be clear" into observed evidence. The [risk analysis](08%20Handover%20Risk%20Analysis.md) names this as the open validation step for the facilitator materials; this is the script for it.
**Rule of the test:** you may hand over the tablet and the printed [Quick Reference card](07%20Facilitator%20Quick%20Reference.md), and then you say nothing. Every time you feel the urge to explain, that urge is a finding. Write down what happened instead.

---

## Setup (before the facilitator arrives)

- The game installed and working on the tablet, NOT pinned, on the Android home screen.
- The printed Quick Reference card (both sides) next to the tablet.
- The leaderboard containing a few team scores (so clearing it is a real act).
- Deliberately mis-set two things, so the test includes real fixes:
  - Set **Hoofdvolume** low (a "no sound" complaint waiting to happen).
  - Tilt-steer with the tablet held at an angle once, so a **KALIBREER** is plausibly needed.
- A notepad for you. You record; you do not coach.

## The script (give these tasks one at a time, in these words)

Say: *"Stel: ik ben er niet meer. Jij draait de activiteit vandaag. Alles wat je nodig hebt ligt hier."* Then give the tasks:

1. **"Maak de tablet klaar voor de dag, zo dat kinderen niet uit het spel kunnen."**
   Pass: opens the game and pins it (screen-pinning) using the card. Watch for: do they find pinning at all?
2. **"Start een groepje en speel zelf een halve beurt."**
   Pass: taps a team chip, tilts to steer, uses gas/brake. Watch for: do they try to type a name?
3. **"Het geluid staat te zacht. Los het op."**
   Pass: side buttons AND settings menu > GELUID. Watch for: do they find the hidden menu gesture from the card?
4. **"Een kind zegt dat het sturen scheef voelt. Los het op."**
   Pass: settings > BESTURING > KALIBREER, holding the tablet correctly while tapping.
5. **"Maak het spel makkelijker voor een jong groepje."**
   Pass: MOEILIJKHEID or VERKEER EN WEG lowered; can say roughly what they changed.
6. **"Er komt een nieuwe klas. Doe wat er dan moet gebeuren."**
   Pass: BEHEER > Klassement wissen, understands the double confirm and that it cannot be undone.
7. **"De dag is voorbij. Sluit af."**
   Pass: unpins (Back + Recents), optionally clears scores, plugs the charger in.
8. Ask, without the tablet: **"Wat doe je als het spel vastloopt terwijl er een klas staat te wachten?"**
   Pass: wait, then close and reopen, then restart the tablet; knows the scores are not lost.

## Scoring (honest, simple)

Per task, record one of: **alone** (no help, card allowed) / **with the card after searching** / **stuck** (needed you). The handover passes when every task is *alone* or *with the card*. Any *stuck* task is a defect in the card or the menu, not in the facilitator; fix the material (reword the card line, rename a setting label) and re-test just that task with the next person, not the same one.

Also record verbatim: the first thing they tap on each task, any wrong guess, and anything they say out loud while searching. Those quotes are the evidence the docs need, and they go into the portfolio write-up of this test.

## What NOT to conclude

One facilitator passing is evidence the materials are usable, not proof for all staff. Do not generalise beyond "one trained-by-nobody staff member ran the day unaided." That is exactly the claim the handover needs, and nothing more. Never invent or embellish the results; write down only what happened.
